Our Programs & Projects

Transforming Lives in Kebri Dahar and the Somali Region

From renovating a historic boarding school to planting fruit trees and building clean restrooms — every project transforms lives in Kebri Dahar and the Somali Region.

Flagship Project

Shaykosh Boarding School

Renovating and upgrading Ethiopia's premier boarding school for pastoralist children — library, dormitories, boundary walls, main gate, and road construction.

  • Library renovation and furnishing
  • Dormitory improvements
  • Boundary wall construction
  • Main gate construction
  • Road construction

Empowerment

Girls' Education

Providing uniforms, school supplies, safe separate sanitation facilities, and a nurturing environment to keep girls in school and thriving.

62 girls currently enrolled at Shaykosh Boarding School

The organization's stated goal is to reach at least 70% female enrollment through targeted support and infrastructure improvements.

  • School uniforms
  • Exercise books, pens and school bags
  • Separate sanitation facilities
  • Safe dormitory spaces
  • Academic support & mentorship
  • Library access

Sustainability

Tree Planting & Greening

Over 1,000 fruit and shade trees have been planted, including mango, papaya, and avocado, alongside vegetable gardens, rainwater harvesting, and full compound greening.

  • Fruit trees — mango, papaya, avocado
  • Shade trees throughout the campus
  • Vegetable gardens
  • Rainwater harvesting systems
  • Greening the school compound

WASH

Water, Sanitation & Hygiene

New modern restrooms for boys and girls, shower facilities, handwashing stations, and expanded water supply for the whole school campus.

  • Modern restrooms for boys
  • Modern restrooms for girls
  • Shower facilities
  • Handwashing stations
  • Expanded water supply
  • New water tanks
  • Water piping to dormitories, classrooms, and WASH facilities

Completed Work on the Ground

Every donation translates directly into tangible improvements that students and teachers see and feel every single day.

Library Renovated & Furnished

Complete renovation of the school library with new shelving, reading tables, chairs, and hundreds of books donated.

New Restrooms & Showers

Separate modern toilet and shower blocks for boys and girls, with handwashing stations at every entrance.

Water Supply Expanded

New water tanks and piping extended to dormitories, classrooms, and WASH facilities across the campus.

1,000+ Trees Planted

Mango, papaya, avocado, and shade trees transforming a bare compound into a lush green campus.

Boundary Wall & Main Gate

New perimeter boundary wall and landmark entrance gate built, securing and beautifying the school campus.

Solar Lighting Installed

Solar-powered lighting for dormitories enabling students to study safely after dark.
