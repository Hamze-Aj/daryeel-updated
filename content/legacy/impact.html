Achievements on the Ground

Every donation translates directly into tangible improvements that students and teachers see and feel every single day.

Library Renovated & Furnished

Complete renovation of the school library with new shelving, reading tables, chairs, and hundreds of books donated.

New Restrooms & Showers

Separate modern toilet and shower blocks for boys and girls, with handwashing stations at every entrance.

Water Supply Expanded

New water tanks and piping extended to dormitories, classrooms, and WASH facilities across the campus.

1,000+ Trees Planted

Mango, papaya, avocado, and shade trees transforming a bare compound into a lush green campus.

Boundary Wall & Main Gate

New perimeter boundary wall and landmark entrance gate built, securing and beautifying the school campus.

Solar Lighting Installed

Solar-powered lighting for dormitories enabling students to study safely after dark.

Girls' Education Impact

62 Girls currently enrolled at Shaykosh Boarding School
~14% Current
70% Goal

Daryeel's goal is to reach at least 70% female enrollment through targeted support and infrastructure improvements.

  • Safe, separate sanitation — dedicated toilets, showers & handwashing
  • Full school uniforms, exercise books, pens and bags
  • Renovated, secure dormitory spaces
  • Academic support & university-volunteer mentorship
  • Library access specifically encouraged for girls
