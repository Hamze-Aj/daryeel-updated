Our Team & Leadership

Clear Hierarchy · Institutional Governance · Field Execution
Executive Leadership Department Heads Operational & Field Staff Advisory Board Organizational Chart

Driven by Diaspora Commitment & Ground Expertise

Daryeel Rural Development For Action operates under a clear, accountable institutional hierarchy. Founded by Somali diaspora members in Minnesota, USA, our leadership works side-by-side with experienced field operations teams and local community authorities in the Somali Region of Ethiopia.
