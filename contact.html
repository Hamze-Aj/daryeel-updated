Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for diaspora members, donors, and organizational partners.

Where is Daryeel located?

Our operational headquarters and flagship project — Shaykosh Boarding School — are in Sheygoash, Kebri Dahar, Somali Region of Ethiopia. Our diaspora leadership team is based in Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA.

How are donations managed?

Daryeel operates with 100% financial transparency. Funds raised from Somali diaspora communities and partners are directly deployed for materials, construction, student meals, and infrastructure in Sheygoash.

Can I visit the school in Sheygoash?

Yes! We warmly welcome diaspora members, supporters, and partners to visit Shaykosh Boarding School. Please contact Eng Hamza in advance to coordinate your visit.

How can I become an institutional partner?

Organizations interested in supporting WASH, environmental greening, or girls' education can reach out via our contact form or email daryeelrural@gmail.com for partnership discussions.
